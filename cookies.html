Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Short answer

This website doesn't set any cookies of its own. We checked our own code directly: there's no analytics, advertising, or tracking script anywhere on this site, and nothing we've written reads or writes cookies.

There are two things on this page that load resources from elsewhere, worth knowing about below.

Our sign-up form (Jotform)

The sign-up/endorsement form in the "Support Chris" section of this site is provided by Jotform Inc. and embedded here, hosted on Jotform's own servers. When you view or use that form, Jotform may set its own cookies — for things like remembering your form progress, keeping the embed secure, and Jotform's own site statistics. These cookies are set by Jotform, not by us, and are governed by Jotform's own policy, which you can read at jotform.com/cookie-policy.

Fonts and the carousel script

This page loads a typeface from Google Fonts and a small script library (Swiper, which runs the endorsements carousel) from a content delivery network called jsDelivr. Neither sets cookies for this purpose, but loading them does mean your browser makes a request to Google's and jsDelivr's servers, which will see your IP address — as happens with any resource loaded from another website.

What this means for you

Because this site itself sets no cookies, there's nothing for you to opt in or out of here — you don't need to accept or decline anything on this page. If you'd rather Jotform didn't set any cookies at all, the only way to be sure of that is not to interact with the sign-up form; you can still get in touch with the campaign by email instead — see our Contact section or email chris.white.cgtd@gmail.com directly.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, email chris.white.cgtd@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Statement for how we handle information submitted through the sign-up form.

This is a plain-English starting draft prepared for the campaign, not legal advice. It should be checked against the Liberal Democrats' own compliance and data protection guidance before being relied on as the campaign's final cookie policy.
