This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, which cookies this website uses, and how you can manage your preferences.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They're widely used to make websites work, to make them work more efficiently, and to provide information to the people who run the site.

Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are required for the site to function and can't be switched off. They don't store any personal information.

Third-party cookies: our sign-up form

If you accept non-essential cookies, this site loads a sign-up and endorsement form provided by Jotform Inc., embedded directly from Jotform's own servers. Loading the form sets the following cookies, among others:

Jotform's own Cookie Policy describes further categories — including preference, statistics, and marketing cookies — that may apply depending on how you use the form. These are set and controlled by Jotform, not by us. Read Jotform's Cookie Policy at jotform.com/cookie-policy.

Campaign video (YouTube)

The video in the "Campaign Video" section is embedded using YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com), which doesn't set cookies, including while the video is playing. Loading or playing it does mean your browser contacts Google's servers, which will see your IP address, as happens with any video player loaded from another website.

Fonts and scripts

This site loads a typeface from Google Fonts and a script library (Swiper, which runs the endorsements carousel) from the jsDelivr content delivery network. Neither sets cookies, but loading them means your browser makes a request to Google's and jsDelivr's servers, which will see your IP address, as happens with any resource loaded from another website.

Managing your cookie preferences

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies using the banner shown when you first visit this site. To change your choice at any time, use the button below.

If you reject non-essential cookies, the sign-up form won't load, and the "Support Chris" section of the homepage will show a message explaining how to allow it if you change your mind.

You can also control cookies through your browser's own settings, including blocking or deleting them. Doing so may affect how parts of this site, including the sign-up form, work.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
