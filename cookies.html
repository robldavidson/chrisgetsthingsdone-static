Cookie Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

Short answer

This website's own code sets no cookies — we checked directly, and there's no analytics, advertising, or tracking script anywhere in it. But this page embeds a sign-up form provided by Jotform, and that embed loads automatically as part of the page loading, before you interact with anything. Jotform sets its own cookies at that point. See below for what we found when we checked this directly, and what it means for you.

Our sign-up form (Jotform)

The sign-up/endorsement form in the "Support Chris" section of this site is provided by Jotform Inc. and embedded here, hosted on Jotform's own servers. The embed isn't behind a click or a "load form" button — it loads automatically as soon as this page does, the same as any image on the page.

We tested this directly: loading the form on its own, with no interaction at all, set two cookies immediately — one recording the page that referred you, one recording your screen size — and showed no cookie-consent prompt of its own. Jotform's published policy describes further categories of cookies (including statistics and marketing) that may also apply depending on how you use the form. These cookies are set by Jotform, not by us, and are governed by Jotform's own policy, which you can read at jotform.com/cookie-policy.

Fonts and the carousel script

This page loads a typeface from Google Fonts and a small script library (Swiper, which runs the endorsements carousel) from a content delivery network called jsDelivr. Neither sets cookies for this purpose, but loading them does mean your browser makes a request to Google's and jsDelivr's servers, which will see your IP address — as happens with any resource loaded from another website.

What this means for you

Because this site's own code sets no cookies, there's nothing for you to opt in or out of that we control. But because the Jotform form loads automatically with the page, its cookies are set as soon as you load this page — not only if you go on to fill in or submit the form. Not interacting with the form doesn't avoid this. If you'd rather avoid Jotform's cookies altogether, block third-party cookies in your browser before visiting, or get in touch with the campaign by email instead without visiting this page — chris.white.cgtd@gmail.com.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, email chris.white.cgtd@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Statement for how we handle information submitted through the sign-up form.

This is a plain-English starting draft prepared for the campaign, not legal advice. It should be checked against the Liberal Democrats' own compliance and data protection guidance before being relied on as the campaign's final cookie policy.
