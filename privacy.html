Privacy Statement

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This privacy statement explains what personal information this website and campaign collect, why we collect it, and what rights you have over it.

Who we are

This site is run by volunteers on behalf of Chris White's campaign to be elected Chair of the Liberal Democrats' English Party. The data controller is C White, 17 Cunningham Avenue, St Albans AL1 1JJ. For any question about this statement or about your information, email chris.white.cgtd@gmail.com.

What information we collect

If you use the sign-up form on this site, we may collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number (optional)
  • Your Liberal Democrat membership number
  • The name of your local party
  • Any endorsement text or photo you choose to submit
  • Whether you're happy to be contacted, and how

We don't collect any other personal information through this site. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers of our own.

How we collect it

All of the above is collected through the sign-up/endorsement form embedded in the "Support Chris" section of this site, which is provided by Jotform Inc. ("Jotform"), our online form provider. When you submit the form, your information goes directly to Jotform's servers and then to us — the page itself doesn't store or process anything.

Jotform, our data processor

Jotform acts as a data processor on our instructions, collecting and storing the information you submit through our sign-up form on our behalf. Jotform may use its own subprocessors to help run its platform, as described in Jotform's own privacy policy. You can read Jotform's privacy policy at jotform.com/privacy and its cookie policy at jotform.com/cookie-policy.

Why we collect it, and our legal basis

We use your information to:

  • Respond to you if you've asked a question or asked to get involved
  • Keep you updated about the campaign — but only if you've told us you're happy to be contacted
  • Consider your endorsement for publication on this site — we'll always check with you individually before publishing your name, photo, or words

Our legal basis for this is your consent, where you've opted in to be contacted, and our legitimate interest in administering a fair and transparent internal party election campaign.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as the campaign is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards to comply with any party reporting requirements, after which we delete it. You can ask us to delete your information sooner at any time — see "Your rights" below.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it with Jotform, as described above, to the extent necessary to operate the sign-up form, and we may share it internally with campaign volunteers helping Chris's campaign. We won't share it with anyone else unless required to by law.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Ask us to restrict how we use it
  • Object to how we use it
  • Ask for a copy of it in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, email chris.white.cgtd@gmail.com. If you're not satisfied with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site uses one strictly necessary cookie to remember your cookie preference, and loads a third-party sign-up form only if you've accepted. See our separate Cookie Policy for full details.

Changes to this statement

We may update this statement from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
